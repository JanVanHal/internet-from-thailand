Limits (read this)

  • Not a Thailand VPS. Traffic leaves your device, not our server.
  • CORS. A “blocked by browser” result does not mean the site is down.
  • no-cors opaque. Success only means a network-level request likely completed; we cannot see HTTP status.
  • Optional relay. If enabled, HTTP status comes from a public CORS proxy’s location — not Thailand and not you. Treat as a hint only.
