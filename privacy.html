Privacy

Short and plain. Updated 7 Oct 2026 (Asia/Bangkok).

What we collect

This v1 site is static HTML/JS. We do not run accounts, logins, or a backend that stores your tool inputs. URLs and domains you type stay in your browser session unless you copy them elsewhere.

Some tools call public third-party endpoints from your browser (for example Cloudflare DNS-over-HTTPS for lookups, or CDN downloads for the approximate speed test). Those providers see a normal HTTPS request from your IP, like any website you visit.

localStorage

Optional preferences (for example last timezone meeting inputs) may be saved in your browser’s localStorage. Clear site data in your browser to remove them. Nothing sensitive (passwords, IDs, payment data) is requested.

Advertising

AdSense is not live in this prototype. HTML comments mark where ad units would go. When ads are enabled, this page will describe cookies and provide an opt-out path as required.

No tracking beyond ads (v1)

No custom analytics pixel is included in this build. Hosting platforms (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, etc.) may keep standard access logs on their side — that is outside this static bundle.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13.
