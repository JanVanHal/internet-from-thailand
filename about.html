Internet from Thailand

About

A small static toolbox for people who live or work between Thailand and elsewhere.

The idea

Jan is Dutch and lives in Thailand. This site is a low-effort side project: useful micro-tools, clean pages, and room for AdSense later — without accounts, apps, or high-liability topics.

Stack preference: single-purpose HTML, vanilla JavaScript, localStorage when useful, CSV if exports appear. No frameworks unless asked.

Honesty policy

  • Client-side checks reflect your network and browser, not a Thailand data centre — unless we ship a real remote probe and label it.
  • Speed numbers are approximate.
  • DNS data comes from public DNS-over-HTTPS (Cloudflare) — public records only.
  • No finance, tax, legal, or medical advice.
